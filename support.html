FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Warm Cafe does not require an account in Release V1.
Is Warm Cafe free?
Yes. Release V1 is free to download and use. No purchases or subscriptions are enabled in this version.
When is Warm Cafe open?
Warm Cafe is open from 6:00 PM to 11:59 PM based on the device local time and US locale.
Why can I see the cafe but not enter a conversation?
If it is outside opening hours or the device region is outside the United States, the cafe remains visible and residents show a short notice when tapped.
How do I clear a conversation?
Open a resident conversation and use the clear chat button in the top bar.
