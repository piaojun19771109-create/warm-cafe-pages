Warm Cafe Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

What Data Is Collected

Warm Cafe Release V1 does not require an account. The app may process the following information to provide conversations:

  • Messages you type in resident conversations.
  • Conversation history stored locally on your device so chats can persist after app restart.
  • Basic app state stored locally, such as whether a first-visit welcome has already been shown.

What Data Is Not Collected

  • No account, name, email address, or password is required.
  • Warm Cafe does not sell personal data.
  • Warm Cafe does not use advertising trackers.
  • Warm Cafe does not collect precise location data.
  • No purchases or subscriptions are enabled in Release V1.

OpenAI API Usage Disclosure

Warm Cafe uses the OpenAI API to generate resident conversation responses. When you send a message, that message and relevant conversation context may be sent to OpenAI to produce a reply.

Warm Cafe uses this processing only to provide the conversation experience inside the app.

Data Retention Policy

Conversation history may be stored locally on your device. You can clear a resident conversation from inside the app.

Warm Cafe does not provide an account-based cloud history system in Release V1.

Contact Email

For privacy questions, contact support@example.com.
